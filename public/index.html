FAQ

What's the difference between carbon and ceramic tint?

Both are metal-free and won't interfere with your phone, GPS, or key fob. Carbon gives a matte look with solid heat and glare reduction. Ceramic is our premium film and rejects the most heat while keeping the view clear.

Will window tint affect my phone or GPS signal?

Carbon and ceramic film are metal-free, so they don't block signals the way metallic films can. If you're unsure about a specific setup, mention it when you book and we'll talk it through.

How long does an install take?

It depends on the vehicle and how much glass is being done. We'll give you an estimate for your car when you book.

When can I roll my windows down?

Both films need 3 to 5 days to cure fully. Keep the windows up during that time, and follow the aftercare instructions we give you at pickup.

Is window tint legal where I live?

Tint laws vary by state and by window. Before you choose a shade, check the current rules for your area. We're happy to help you find a film that fits within them.
