Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Midnight Tinting collects when you contact us or book an appointment, how we use it, and your choices.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Midnight Tinting provides mobile automotive window tint in Franklin Park and across Cook and DuPage County, Illinois. You can reach us at info@midnight-tint.com or (708) 677-9815.

What we collect

When you request an appointment, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your vehicle's year, make, and model
  • The film and package you choose, your preferred date and start time, and any notes you add

When you visit the website, Google Analytics collects general usage information, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive. We do not ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

  • To review your request, confirm your appointment time, and contact you about it
  • To hold your appointment time on our calendar
  • To send you a receipt of your request and reply to your questions
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the website and booking system:

  • Google (Google Calendar and Google Analytics)
  • Resend, which sends booking emails
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the website and stores booking requests

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and to keep our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@midnight-tint.com. You can also limit cookies in your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
